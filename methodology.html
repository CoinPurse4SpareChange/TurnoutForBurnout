Methods and limitations

How we distinguish evidence from interpretation and avoid overstating psychological findings.

Editorial commitments

  • Distinguish observational associations from causal conclusions.
  • Specify when findings derive from sectors other than public education.
  • Distinguish documented organizational behavior from employees’ perceptions.
  • Avoid treating contested terms—such as moral injury—as interchangeable with medical diagnoses.
  • Preserve student rights and educator safety as simultaneous concerns.
  • Seek counterevidence before making strong claims.

Evidence labels

Direct evidence: research involving educators and the specific relationship discussed. Adjacent evidence: research from other occupations or related outcomes. Hypothesis: a proposed causal pathway that requires testing.

Current limitations

This version is an initial synthesis. It does not conduct a systematic search, evaluate all study quality, or establish how often institutional fear of litigation contributes to teacher mental health outcomes. Those are priorities for later revisions.

Corrections and updates

The project is intended for version control in GitHub so subsequent evidence changes can be transparently documented. Revision date: October 15, 2026.
